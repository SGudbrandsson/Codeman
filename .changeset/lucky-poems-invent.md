Selecting text in a transcript message or a markdown document now shows a
"Play from here" pill, making the start-from-a-point behaviour visible instead
of hidden behind the play button. If a read-aloud session is already running
and the selected block belongs to it, the pill seeks rather than restarting.
Selection pills now also lift clear of the read-aloud playback bar, the compose
panel and the mobile keyboard accessory, which previously overlapped them.
